# Redmond

The **Redmond** district of [Seattle](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Seattle), [UCAS](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/UCAS) is more commonly known as **The Redmond [Barrens](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Barrens)**. Well known for its run-down [metahuman](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Metahumans) ghettos/projects.

## Overview

Redmond is the definition of urban decay. Due to the partial meltdown of the Trojan-Satsop nuclear power plant in 2013, Beaver Lake and kilometers of its surroundings have been contaminated resulting in Glow City. Many of its residents fled, and more left after the first crash of 2029 collapsed the computer industry. In the aftermath homeless squatters moved into the district and the government left it to the criminals. Of it's residents, approximately 3/4 of them are SINless. The district lacks sanitation, running water, and electricity apart from what one can jury-rig for themselves and a functional roof is unknown to most.^[[1]](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Redmond#cite_note-1)

Redmond is virtually lawless, except in the areas that border other districts. Redmond is also known for its notorious gang fights and violence, scaring many away from the area. Best known gangs of Redmond are the "[Red Hot Nukes](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Hot_Nukes)" ([dwarfs](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Dwarf)), the "[Rusted Stilettos](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Rusted_Stilettos)" (merely [orks](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Ork) and [trolls](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Troll)), the "[162s](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/162s)" ([ghouls](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Ghoul)) and the "[Brain Eaters](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Brain_Eaters)". Other gangs in the Redmond Barrens are the "[Spiders](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Spiders)" - known for their fanatical hatred of [Insect Spirits](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Insect_Spirits) - and the "[Crimson Crush](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Crimson_Crush)"-gang that calls the area east of Touristville around 228th Avenue their home turf.

## Geography

Redmond is relatively flat, with a few small hills. [Snoqualme River](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Snoqualme_River) passes through a third of the district.

Redmond borders [Snohomish](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Snohomish) in the north, [Bellevue](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Bellevue) in the west, and [Renton](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Renton) in the south. Its eastern border is with the [Salish-Shidhe Council](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Salish-Shidhe_Council).

The border with Bellevue is mostly walled and guarded to prevent "undesirable" elements spilling into that luxury district over.

## Demographics

Official statistics estimate Redmond at about half a million; unofficial go as high as 700,000. The number of metahumans is lower than the average for the Seattle area.

## Politics

Mayor [Jeffrey Gasston](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Jeffrey_Gasston) was elected in [2048](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/2048).

[2070s](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/2070s): Mayor [Sonya Scholl](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Sonya_Scholl) has managed to remain in office because no one wants to spend the nuyen on the bullet to take her out. [Danny Williams](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Danny_Williams) and [Rebecca Lewis](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Rebecca_Lewis) are the favorite challengers.

## Economy

What little economy is here is unregulated and dominated by the criminal elements.

The only part of Redmond, which isn't completely a slum is known as [Touristville](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Touristville). In this area lots of bars, clubs and discos, controlled by [Mafia](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Mafia), [Yakuza](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Yakuza) and the [Seoulpa Rings](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Seoulpa_Rings) try to extract money from people, which come there, looking for cheap amusement like gambling, prostitution, drugs, chips and alcohol.

## Crime

Redmond has more gangs than any other district in Seattle, hundreds, in fact. The gangs are the closest thing to a law or a government. The [Rusted Stilettos](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Rusted_Stilettos) claim territory near Glow City, and run protection rackets there. Many of the [Trolls](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Troll) and [Orks](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Ork) who make up the gang are literally mutants, with sickly looks and pale skin. Their colours are black and rust red. The [Brain Eaters](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Brain_Eaters) are a mixed-race gang operating in the Touristville area. They scavenge what remains of Redmond's technology, and are the sole group of shadow-deckers operating out of Redmond. They are also heavily into smuggling.

The [Red Hot Nukes](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Hot_Nukes) are a [dwarf](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Dwarf) [go-gang](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Go-gang) based around Route 202. They consider themselves more of a neighbourhood association than a gang, and are explosives experts. Most of their rackets involve demolitions in some way; The Nukes love to blow things up. Their colours are gray and red, and all members wear baseball caps.

The [Spiders](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Spiders) are one of Redmond's largest and newest gangs, made up of victims of the [Universal Brotherhood](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Universal_Brotherhood). They all follow the [Spider totem](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_totem), even the un-Awakened, and are led by a Spider [shaman](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Shaman) named Widow. The Spiders are obsessed with protecting Redmond from [insect spirits](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Insect_spirit) and other magical threats, and press-gang people into the gang from time to time. They are headquartered just south of Beaver Lake and Glow City, and their colours are black or dark brown with a red web pattern overlaid.
