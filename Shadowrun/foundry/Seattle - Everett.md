# Everett

**Everett** is the northern district of the [Seattle](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Seattle) Metroplex. The Everett Naval Ship Yards are located there.

Everett is similar to its “sister city,” [Tacoma](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Tacoma). Both are important ports with busy waterfronts, and are also important centers of organized crime in Seattle.

## Geography

To the north Everett is bounded by the lands of [Salish-Shidhe Council](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Salish-Shidhe_Council), on the west by [Puget Sound](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Puget_Sound), and on the east and south by [Snohomish](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Snohomish).

## Politics

[2048](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/2048) saw the election of [Samantha Tillian](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Samantha_Tillian).

[2072](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/2072): Everett Mayor [Matthew Hilcox](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Matthew_Hilcox) presides over a district in dire need of more leadership than he can offer it. Hilcox rides on the record of former Mayor Samantha Tillian, aiming at bringing business back into Everett. Unfortunately for the district, he was bought and paid for by those same business interests, who want to ensure they get the best deals when it comes to disputed properties. Mayor Hilcox’s major roadblock is the UCAS federal government, which wants additional land for the Naval Shipyard, and doesn’t much care about the mayor’s business interests.

[2076](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/2076) : Mayor [Matthew Hilcox](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Matthew_Hilcox) keeps fighting the [Navy](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/UCAS_Navy), and he keeps losing, but he sees it as a roadblock to Everett’s future. The majority of the council, however, loves the government’s encouragement and money.

## Crime

Everett is under the control of the [Mafia](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Mafia), with some presence of [Yakuza](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Yakuza) and [Seoulpa Rings](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Seoulpa_Rings). More recently, the Triads have taken over. It sees considerable smuggling activity by land and sea. The district is also home to one of Seattle’s larger-scale gang conflicts, the war between the [Choson Ring](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Choson_Ring) and the [First Nations](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/First_Nations). Other major gangs include [Kabuki Ronin](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuki_Ronin), [Razor Heads](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Razor_Heads), [Red Rovers](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Rovers), the [Scatterbrains](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Scatterbrains).
