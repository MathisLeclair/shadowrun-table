# Downtown

**Seattle Downtown** is a major cultural center of the town. Its full of shops, hotels, cinemas, and such.

Formerly the city [Seattle](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Seattle), [USA](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/USA). Now located in [Seattle State](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Seattle), [UCAS](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/UCAS).

Downtown Seattle is where things are happening. The hub of the metroplex, this district is the home of many of the major corporations and government factions likely to employ [shadowrunners](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Shadowrunner). Downtown also has the plex’s trendiest nightclubs and places to see and be seen.

## Geography

Downtown is fairly compact compared to other city centers on the West Coast because of its geographical situation: hemmed in on the north and east by hills, on the west by the Elliott Bay, and on the south by reclaimed land that was once tidal flats.

Major neighborhoods: [Arcology Commercial and Housing Enclave](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Arcology_Commercial_and_Houcing_Enclave) (ACHE, formerly the [Renraku Arcology](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Renraku_Arcology)), [Ballard](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Ballard), [Capitol Hill](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Capitol_Hill), [Council Island](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Council_Island), [Elven District](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Elven_District), [International District](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/International_District), [Ork Underground](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Ork_Underground), [Queen Anne Hill, Interbay and Magnolia Bluff](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Queen_Anne_Hill,_Interbay_and_Magnolia_Bluff), [Seattle Center](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Seattle_Center), [University District](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/University_District).

## Politics

The 2048 elections saw the rise of mayor [Ivar J. Lindstorm](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Ivar_J._Lindstorm).

[2072](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/2072): [Nikola Taul](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Nikola_Taul), the charming new mayor of Downtown, comes from a Russian immigrant family that has been in the Seattle area since before the founding of the metroplex. She served as a member of the Downtown District Council for twelve years before her run for the mayor’s office, on a platform of cleaning up Downtown and restoring “the jewel of the Emerald City” to peak condition. Mayor Taul knows her way around Metroplex Hall quite well, and she has been enthusiastically pursuing the work of sprucing up Downtown, tapping whatever metroplex funds she can to do it. Seattle media has adopted “Downtown Taking Its Taul” as a catchphrase for the mayor’s new programs and their cost

[2076](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/2076) [Mayor Nikola Taul](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Nikola_Taul) is focused entirely on her gubernatorial campaign, which has left a vacuum where the council fights over the fate of the [ACHE](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/ACHE) (and whether it deserves political representation) and the gentrification of the [Ork Underground](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Ork_Underground) by dwarves from the Elven District, which has resulted in a counter-movement by orks and trolls into the neighborhood.

## Economy

Downtown accounts for about half of Seattle's economy, primarily in the form of the site of corporate headquarters, retail, tourism and transport.

## Culture

A number of major Seattle's cultural institutions are present here - museums, event halls, prominent bars, clubs and restaurants, and so on.

## Crime

Both [Mafia](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Mafia) and [Yakuza](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Yakuza) have a significant presence Downtown, and are rumored to have their headquarters in this district.

Major gangs are present here, including the [Bloody Screamers](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Bloody_Screamers), [Disassemblers](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Disassemblers), [First Nation](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/First_Nation), [Halloweeners](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Halloweeners), [Troll Killers](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Troll_Killers).
