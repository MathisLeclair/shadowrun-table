# Renton

**Renton** is one of the most "average" districts in Seattle - which isn't saying much. It's primarily a middle-low class residential neighborhood.

Renton’s problems are less serious than those of the [Redmond](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Redmond) Barrens, but the district has definitely seen better days. Renton has more gangs than any other area in Seattle except Redmond and is also a hotbed of [Humanis Policlub](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Humanis_Policlub) activity, making anti-metahuman sentiment all too common.

## Geography

Renton is surrounded by [Bellevue](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Bellevue) and [Redmond](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Redmond) in the north, [Downtown Seattle](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Downtown_Seattle) and [Tacoma](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Tacoma) in the west, [Auburn](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Auburn) in the south, and [Salish-Shidhe Council](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Salish-Shidhe_Council) lands in the east.

## Demographics

About 35% of Renton's population is metahuman.

## Politics

The [2048](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/2048) elections saw the rise of mayor [Carl B. Vandersalm](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Carl_B._Vandersalm) for his third term.

[Humanis Policlub](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Humanis_Policlub) Seattle headquarters are located here.

[2052](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/2052)-[2072](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/2072): Samuel “Smilin’ Sam” Usinski is one of the senior mayors of the metroplex, having served as Mayor of Renton for twenty years now, replacing old Carl Vanderslam. Usinski’s terms in office have focused heavily on “safeguarding” Renton from a variety of dangers, ranging from gangs and chips to corporate conflicts and the second computer Crash. Every election, Renton’s attitude seems to be “better the devil you know.” Usinski is also a major [Brackhaven](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Kenneth_Brackhaven) supporter, so the governor is sure to throw a little extra Renton’s way to shore up the mayor’s polling numbers and keep him right where he is.

[2076](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/2076): Renton is seeing a number of conflicts as the demographics change. Transplants from the east and south, many of them metahumans, mean that the racism remains but is becoming more subtle. With an ork and dwarf now on the council, policing principles may change.

The [Apep Consortium](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Apep_Consortium) library was recently completed near [Cougar Mountain](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Cougar_Mountain). Newly elected Mayor [Jeremy Lucas](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Jeremy_Lucas) seems intent on challenging the bunker mentality of the locals.

## Economy

Renton's features many of Seattle's commercial shops aiming at middle and low class customers.

It is also notable for a number of security industry and software industry facilities.

## Crime

Mafia and Yakuza are highly active here.

Major gangs include the [Blood Mountain Boys](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Mountain_Boys) (go-gang) and the [Night Hunters](http://shadowrun.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Hunters) (thrill gang).
